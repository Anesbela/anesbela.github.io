1. Data Collection and Usage

Local Storage: All data entered into PAWSULIN, including insulin doses, injection sites, and timestamps, is stored strictly locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not transmit, collect, or store your personal or pet's medical data on any external servers.

We use Google AdMob to display ads in the App. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies or unique device identifiers to serve ads based on your previous visits to this App or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses collected data by visiting the Google AdMob Privacy Policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.
